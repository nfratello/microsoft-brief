Safer student AI and what the best cities do differently

Morning. Microsoft shipped a lot this week. I kept the part that matters to public sector, and it fits in a few minutes.

This period brought new controls for safer student AI and fresh research showing how people-first cities are getting more out of AI.

 
 
Can student AI stay focused on learning?
What do cities getting double the AI return do differently?
How are Broward teachers getting 6–7 hours back a week?
 
1 big thing

Schools can soon lock Copilot into tutor mode

What's new: A coming IT setting keeps students inside the Study and Learn tutoring agent. They can't leave it for general Copilot Chat or other agents, but can still attach their own files.

Why it matters for public sector: Districts that hesitated over open-ended AI chat get a learning-only mode run by IT. Students get coaching that makes them do the thinking.
Source: Microsoft Education Blog · Nov 2
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Cities that put residents first earn double AI returns

What's new: ThoughtLab research on 200+ cities finds leaders report a 99% average AI return, against 42% for other cities. Examples include Raleigh's traffic digital twin, built in six weeks.

Why it matters for public sector: City and county leaders get benchmark data plus a practical approach: start with residents' needs, build in security, and keep sensitive data under local control.
Source: The Microsoft Cloud Blog · Nov 3

One move for Public communications · State, Local & Education

Rewrite a public notice in plain language

Residents and families skim. Plain language and a translation make notices land the first time.

Copy this prompt
Rewrite this public notice (pasted below) in plain language at an 8th-grade reading level. Keep every date, place and deadline exactly as written. Then give me a Spanish version for a human translator to review.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
