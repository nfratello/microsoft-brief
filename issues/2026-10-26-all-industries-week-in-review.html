This week's stories

 
Copilot

A Copilot built for month-end close

What's new: Microsoft added 25+ finance skills and partner plugins, including account reconciliation and journal entries. In seven days of limited preview, 8,000+ users ran the accounting skills more than 44,000 times.

Why it matters: Finance teams can hand off close, forecast reviews and deal analysis to Copilot, with an optional audit trail of every change.

Source: Microsoft Copilot Blog
 
Dynamics 365

Your CRM, ready for any agent

What's new: The Dynamics 365 Sales MCP server is generally available, giving agents 59 governed tools for leads, opportunities, quotes and orders under your existing security roles.

Why it matters: Sellers can update CRM and turn quotes into orders from Copilot or their own agents, with no custom integration and no change to security.
Read it →
Source: Dynamics 365 Blog
 
Azure

The engine behind Copilot, now yours to build on

What's new: The orchestration layer behind Copilot Studio and GitHub Copilot is in public preview in Microsoft Foundry, with 20+ frontier models and bring-your-own-key.

Why it matters: Teams building their own agents can use the same managed agent loop Microsoft uses, and switch models without rebuilding.
Read it →
Source: Official Microsoft Blog
 
Security

Find the AI agents already on your laptops

What's new: Microsoft Defender can now discover AI agents and MCP servers running on employee devices, now generally available.

Why it matters: You can't govern what you can't see. This shows security teams the unsanctioned AI tools on their endpoints.
Read it →
Source: Microsoft Defender Blog
