Ignite 2026: what it means for telecom and media

 
    

3½-min read · 816 words

Morning. Ignite shipped more news than anyone can read in a week. Here is what matters, in a few minutes.

For carriers, media and gaming companies, Ignite brought AI to the contact center, the sales floor and the network operations team.

 
 
Dynamics 365 in Copilot. Contact Center Intelligence layers AI agents on existing contact centers, so care teams can handle more without replacing platforms.
Microsoft Perception is generally available. Security agents that triage about 80% of alerts help protect large networks and subscriber data around the clock.
Copilot becomes the place work gets done. Marketing, content and care teams can work across Word, Excel and PowerPoint inside one Copilot home.
 
1 big thing

Dynamics 365 in Copilot

What's new: Dynamics 365 now brings 57+ CRM skills for sales and service, and 39+ ERP skills, into Copilot. The Sales and ERP apps plugins are generally available, bringing account planning, deal insights, purchase orders and finance tasks into Copilot and Excel. Dynamics 365 data now flows into Work IQ, and Activate speeds moves from Salesforce, with SAP ECC in private preview.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Contact Center Intelligence layers AI agents on existing contact centers, so care teams can handle more without replacing platforms.
Source: Jeff Teper, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Blog · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Microsoft Perception is generally available

What's new: Red, blue and green team security agents find weaknesses, triage alerts and investigate attacks. The Triage Agent covers about 80% of alerts in a typical Defender queue.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Security agents that triage about 80% of alerts help protect large networks and subscriber data around the clock.
Source: Book of News · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Copilot becomes the place work gets done

What's new: Home brings Chat and Cowork together, the Word, Excel and PowerPoint apps run inside Copilot, and Auto picks the right model for each task. Copilot Code lets anyone build apps in plain language, and Autopilot, a long-running personal agent, is rolling out in Frontier.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Marketing, content and care teams can work across Word, Excel and PowerPoint inside one Copilot home.
Source: Jared Spataro, Microsoft Copilot Blog · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 4

The cloud and data foundation

What's new: Azure Cobalt 200 virtual machines (up to 50% better CPU performance), Azure HorizonDB and Azure Catalyst are generally available.

Why it matters for telecom and media: More efficient compute and agentic operations help run AI at carrier scale.
Source: Book of News · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
