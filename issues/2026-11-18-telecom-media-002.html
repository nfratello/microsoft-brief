1 big thing

KT's AI agents find network faults in minutes

What's new: KT and Microsoft forward-deployed engineers built AIO-Net, an agent platform on Microsoft Foundry trained by KT's veteran network experts. It now detects network issues in 2 to 3 minutes, down from over 10, and agents can only run read-only commands.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Operators can capture veteran engineers' know-how before it retires, and keep humans in charge by limiting agents to read-only actions.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 11

Story 2

Court limits secret demands for journalists' data

What's new: The U.S. Fourth Circuit placed limits on secret government demands for journalists' LinkedIn data and sent the case back for narrower review. Microsoft is now asking Congress to strengthen limits on nondisclosure orders.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Media organizations rely on confidential sources. The ruling supports narrower data demands and gives publishers a clearer legal footing when they choose cloud platforms.
Source: Microsoft On the Issues · Nov 11
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

500 AI projects in, here is what works

What's new: Microsoft Frontier Company has engaged on nearly 500 projects with over 200 customers in 28 countries. Examples include a factory digital twin at Unilever, an order-management agent at 3M, and AIO-Net at KT.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Publicis is co-building agents grounded in its own data and client knowledge. Agencies and media firms could use the same approach to turn their know-how into reusable AI skills.
