1 big thing

Microsoft Classroom brings teaching tools together

What's new: Microsoft Classroom is a new standalone Microsoft 365 space that brings Assignments, Grades, Class Notebooks, Insights and AI tools like Teach together. It will come at no extra cost in current A1, A3, A5 and A7 plans, with a preview planned for early 2027.

Why it matters for public sector: Schools and universities can give teachers and students one home for classwork and grades without replacing Teams or their LMS.
Source: Microsoft Tech Community · Nov 11
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Ohio State and Microsoft sign five-year deal

What's new: Ohio State and Microsoft signed a five-year partnership spanning a smart hospital model, cloud, data and AI skills. The aim is to move about 70% to 80% of storage and compute to Azure and build a unified data foundation on Microsoft Fabric.

Why it matters for public sector: Universities can link research, clinical care and AI skills on one data foundation, and expand certification paths for students.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 10
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Copilot Cowork goes live in GCC

What's new: Copilot Cowork is now generally available in the Government Community Cloud, with GCC High to follow. It runs inside the GCC boundary on government-hosted models and pauses for user approval before it sends email or posts.

Why it matters for public sector: State and local agencies on GCC can hand off multi-step work while keeping their existing permissions, Purview controls and approvals.
Source: Microsoft Tech Community · Nov 18
