1 big thing

Copilot now remembers how you work

What's new: New memory and context in Work IQ let Copilot carry your preferences and past chats forward, so you stop re-explaining your role. Users and admins can review or switch it off.

Why it matters for hospitality: Revenue, sales and property teams stop re-briefing Copilot on brands, rate rules and tone, so answers fit your service standards faster.
Source: Microsoft Copilot Blog · Nov 18
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Copilot can now read your business apps

What's new: Work IQ now grounds Copilot and agents in Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data, generally available. Agents follow your business steps and update records only within each user's permissions.

Why it matters for hospitality: Front desk and reservations teams could ask Copilot about bookings, groups and loyalty records held in Dynamics 365 or Power Apps.
Source: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Blog · Nov 18
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

How Gulf Air tested its agents before going live

What's new: Gulf Air launched voice and chat agents in its Falcon Eye operations app on Nov 9. The team built about 10,000 test questions and fixed gaps in batches before users saw them.

Why it matters for hospitality: An airline's own lesson: test agents against the hard operational questions leaders ask, then fix and retest before launch.
Source: Microsoft Foundry Blog · Nov 20
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
One move for Guest experience · Hospitality & Travel

Turn 50 guest reviews into a fix list

Reading reviews one by one hides the pattern. Copilot can group them in seconds so you fix the biggest issue first.

Copy this prompt
Here are 50 recent guest reviews (pasted below). Group the complaints into themes with a count for each, quote one example per theme, and draft a short, warm reply template for the biggest theme.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
