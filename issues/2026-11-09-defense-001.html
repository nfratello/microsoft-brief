1 big thing

Isolated cloud workspaces for defense programs

What's new: Azure Enclave is a managed service in Azure and Azure for US Government that sets up isolated, policy-governed environments for work with contractors, labs and partners, including the defense industrial base.

Why it matters for defense industry: Program teams can collaborate on sensitive work without a custom build each time. FedRAMP, IL4–6 and ICD 503 accreditation is still in progress.

Story 2

A 90-day AI governance plan for defense contractors

What's new: The final part of Microsoft's series sets out how to govern AI agents in GCC High: an owner for each agent, risk-tiered approvals, containment controls ready in advance, and a 30/60/90-day plan.

Why it matters for defense industry: DIB firms must show auditors who approved each agent and what it can reach. This ties those answers to NIST 800-171 and CMMC work they already do.
