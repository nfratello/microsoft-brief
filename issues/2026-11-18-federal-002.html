1 big thing

A VA analyst builds a benefits agent for veterans

What's new: A Department of Veterans Affairs analyst and former Marine built Battle Buddy, an AI agent that helps veterans and families navigate federal, state and local benefits. He built it in Copilot Studio without being a professional developer, and it is now in VA-backed field testing.

Why it matters for federal: Agencies can empower citizen developers who know the mission to build agents, with governance, licensing and support planned from the start.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 9
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Copilot Cowork goes live in GCC

What's new: Copilot Cowork is now generally available in the Government Community Cloud, with GCC High to follow. It runs inside the GCC boundary on government-hosted models and pauses for user approval before it sends email or posts.

Why it matters for federal: Federal teams on GCC can delegate multi-step work inside their boundary, with Purview, eDiscovery and approval checkpoints in place.
Source: Microsoft Tech Community · Nov 18
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Patch faster as AI finds more flaws

What's new: Microsoft's IT team explained how it keeps software current as AI speeds up how fast flaws are found. Devices must restart and apply OS updates within two days, with Windows Autopatch, Hotpatch and Intune doing most of the work.

Why it matters for federal: Agencies face the same surge in vulnerabilities. Firm deadlines and automated updates could shrink exposure windows across large device fleets.
Source: Microsoft Inside Track · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
One move for Program management · Federal Government

Boil a long report down to a one-page brief

Leaders need the findings and the decisions, not 40 pages. Use only your agency's approved Copilot environment.

Copy this prompt
I've attached a 40-page program report. Write a one-page brief for leadership: 3 key findings, 3 risks, and 3 decisions we need, each with the page number it came from.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
