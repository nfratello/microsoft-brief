This week's stories

 

Describe an app, get an app

What's new: Copilot Code turns plain-language requests into working apps and dashboards using your Work IQ context, and can publish to a managed runtime or hand off to GitHub for engineering review.

Why it matters: Business users can build trackers and decision tools without a dev budget, and shared apps inherit your existing governance.
Source: Microsoft Copilot Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Copilot now understands your business apps

What's new: Business applications in Work IQ entered public preview, alongside business skills in Power Apps, a Business Apps plugin for Cowork and the Dataverse Ask API for plain-language questions over business data.

Why it matters: Agents can work from the Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data and rules you already have, instead of rebuilding context for each one.
Source: Power Platform Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Agents that can use any website

What's new: Browser automation in Microsoft Foundry is now generally available: agents get managed cloud browsers, and Live View lets a person watch and take over.

Why it matters: Agents can finally work in legacy portals and vendor sites that have no API, with people still in control.
Source: Microsoft Foundry Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Treat every agent like a new insider

What's new: Microsoft expanded local-agent discovery to 60 types, including Claude Code, Codex and GitHub Copilot, and gave each agent its own identity with task-scoped permissions.

Why it matters: Security teams can see which AI agents are running and limit what each can do before a mistake or a manipulated agent causes harm.
Source: Microsoft Security Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
