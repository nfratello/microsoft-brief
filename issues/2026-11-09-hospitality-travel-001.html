AI agents for your reservations center, AI assistants that book travel, and who can reach guest data.
Hospitality & Travel edition · Monday, November 9, 2026

Good morning. Copilot is taking on more of the back office, and AI agents are moving into the places travel brands care about most: the reservations line, guest data and the booking path. Here's what changed and why it matters.
This week in 30 seconds

Copilot can now help your finance team close the month faster.

3
Know which AI agents can reach guest and payment data.
 
Microsoft 365 Copilot → For: Finance · Hotel groups · Airlines
Month-end close with Copilot doing the legwork
What's new: Microsoft added 25+ finance skills and partner plugins to Copilot, including account reconciliation and journal entries. In seven days of limited preview, 8,000+ users ran the accounting skills more than 44,000 times.
Why it matters for travel: Travel finance teams close the books across many properties, routes and currencies. Handing reconciliations and variance reviews to Copilot frees analysts for pricing and demand decisions, with an optional audit trail of every change.

Copilot Studio → For: Operations · Guest services
Agents that remember and start work on their own
What's new: Copilot Studio added business process memory, triggers and agent accounts you can @-mention in Teams and Outlook (private preview).
Why it matters for travel: Picture an agent that notices an irregular-operations event or a large group booking and starts the follow-up itself, instead of waiting for someone to ask.

Dynamics 365 → For: Airlines · Hotels · Cruise
Add AI agents to your reservations center without replacing it
What's new: Microsoft Contact Center Intelligence is in public preview. It layers AI self-service, quality-review agents and voice authentication onto the contact center you already run, including Five9, Cisco and Avaya.
Why it matters for travel: Disruption days flood reservations lines. Self-service that scales with the spike, and every call reviewed for quality, can cut wait times without a platform migration.

Security → For: IT · Risk · Loyalty data
See every AI agent that can reach guest data
What's new: Microsoft Defender can now discover AI agents and the tools they connect to on employee devices. It is generally available.
Why it matters for travel: Travel brands hold passports, payment cards and loyalty profiles. An inventory of the AI agents that can touch them is the first step to governing them.

Microsoft Clarity For: Hotels · Airlines · Digital
Can a traveler's AI assistant actually book with you?
What's new: Clarity now shows what AI agents do on your website and tests whether they can finish key tasks, starting with commerce and travel.
Why it matters for travel: Travelers are starting to hand trip planning to AI assistants. If one cannot find or book your rooms, fares or sailings, that booking goes elsewhere and you never see it.
