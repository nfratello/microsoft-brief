Copilot learns law and accounting, inside Word and Excel

 
    

Smart Brevity count: 609 words · 2½ min

Morning. Microsoft shipped a lot this week. I kept the part that matters to professional services, and it fits in a few minutes.

Microsoft launched dedicated Copilot skills for lawyers and accountants this month, bringing AI built for their profession into Word and Excel.

 
 
What changes when legal AI lives inside Word?
Could busy season close faster with an audit trail?
How did PwC save $150M of time with Copilot?
 
1 big thing

Copilot for legal is now generally available

What's new: Microsoft released 20+ legal skills for Copilot: drafting, playbook review, tracked redlines, obligation tracking and case chronologies, plus plugins for iManage, NetDocuments, Harvey and Relativity.

Why it matters for professional services: Law firms already work in Word. Legal-grade AI that edits as native tracked changes and connects to document systems means less switching and faster first drafts.
Source: Microsoft Tech Community · Oct 15
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

New Copilot skills for accountants and deal teams

What's new: Microsoft added 25+ finance skills and plugins for reconciliations, journal entries, audit, tax, valuation models and investment memos. In Excel, users can turn on an audit trail.

Why it matters for professional services: Accounting and advisory firms spend busy seasons on repetitive close and analysis work. Reviewable AI with an audit trail fits how regulated professionals work.
Source: Microsoft Copilot Blog · Oct 29

PwC brings Copilot to its global workforce

PwC moved 364,000 people in 136 countries onto Microsoft 365 with Copilot and built custom agents in Copilot Studio, reporting $150M in time savings from Copilot.

 
One move for Client lead · Professional Services

Walk into every client meeting prepared

Prep means digging through weeks of email. Copilot can pull it together in a minute when it can see your work data.

Copy this prompt
Help me prepare for my meeting with [client] on [date]. From our recent emails and meetings, summarize where things stand, list open questions and decisions, and suggest 3 talking points. One page.

Where it works: Needs Microsoft 365 Copilot: it reads your email and meetings. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
