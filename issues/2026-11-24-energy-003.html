1 big thing

Copilot now remembers how you work

What's new: New memory and context in Work IQ let Copilot carry your preferences and past chats forward, so you stop re-explaining your role. Users and admins can review or switch it off.

Why it matters for energy: Engineers and field leads spend less time re-briefing Copilot on assets, sites and reporting formats.

Story 2

Copilot can now read your business apps

What's new: Work IQ now grounds Copilot and agents in Dynamics 365 and Power Platform data, generally available. Agents follow your business steps and update records only within each user's permissions.

Why it matters for energy: Partner Velrada showed mining shift handovers: incoming crews ask Copilot for risks and open actions from site apps.
Source: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Blog · Nov 18
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

One view of every agent, model and tool

What's new: Foundry adds built-in governance: an identity for every agent, policies for approved models and tools, cost controls, and a fleet view of activity and compliance across projects.

Why it matters for energy: As agents spread across sites and contractors, energy firms can see owners, tools and spend in one place.

One move for Finance · Energy & Utilities

Find the outliers in maintenance spend

Variance reviews eat days. Let Copilot find the big gaps first so you spend your time on the why.

Copy this prompt
I've attached this quarter's maintenance spend by site and our plan. Compare actual to plan, list the 5 biggest variances with likely reasons, and format it as a table I can paste into my CFO update.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
