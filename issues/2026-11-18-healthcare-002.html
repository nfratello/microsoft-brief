1 big thing

Ohio State and Microsoft sign five-year deal

What's new: Ohio State and Microsoft signed a five-year partnership spanning a smart hospital model, cloud, data and AI skills. The aim is to move about 70% to 80% of storage and compute to Azure and build a unified data foundation on Microsoft Fabric.

Why it matters for healthcare: It is a model for academic medical centers: modernize cloud and data first, then apply AI to staffing, surgical demand and research.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 10
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Apollo's AI agent speeds patient safety reviews

What's new: Apollo Hospitals and Microsoft engineers built the Daily Tiered Huddle agent on Microsoft Foundry and Fabric to support patient safety reviews. Apollo's records show over 60% of reviewed cases are now closed within three days.

Why it matters for healthcare: Hospitals can spend less time gathering records and more time preventing harm. An agent that learns from daily huddles could also spot safety trends earlier.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Dragon Copilot can now search your work files

What's new: Work IQ is now generally available in Dragon Copilot, so clinicians can pull relevant email, Teams and file information into Dragon Copilot Chat. Answers link back to their sources and respect existing Microsoft 365 permissions.

Why it matters for healthcare: Clinicians could find on-call schedules, trial criteria and policies without leaving the clinical workflow, with sources to verify each answer.
Source: Microsoft Tech Community · Nov 16

Intermountain scales ambient AI across its Epic workflow

Intermountain Health embedded Dragon Copilot in Epic to capture visits and draft notes. It now has over 2,500 active users, with faster documentation and less clinician burnout.

 
One move for Clinical operations · Healthcare & Life Sciences

Turn a 20-page policy into a one-page checklist

Long policies rarely get read at the start of a shift. A checklist does. Always have a clinical lead review the result.

Copy this prompt
I've attached our updated discharge policy. Turn it into a one-page checklist for charge nurses: 10 steps in order, each one line, with the policy section number in brackets. Flag anything that changed from the last version.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
