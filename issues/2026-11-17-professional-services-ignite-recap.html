Ignite 2026: what it means for professional services

 
    

3½-min read · 841 words

Morning. Ignite shipped more news than anyone can read in a week. Here is what matters, in a few minutes.

For consulting, legal, accounting and engineering firms, Ignite made Copilot the place client work gets done, and showed how to measure what AI returns.

 
 
Copilot becomes the place work gets done. Home, Workspaces and Autopilot let teams draft, analyze and keep client projects moving in one shared space.
Dynamics 365 in Copilot. Project, finance and sales skills in Copilot help partners plan accounts and track work without switching apps.
Agent 365: FinOps for AI. Firms can see AI cost by practice and measure the value it adds to billable work.
 
1 big thing

Copilot becomes the place work gets done

What's new: Home brings Chat and Cowork together, the Word, Excel and PowerPoint apps run inside Copilot, and Auto picks the right model for each task. Copilot Code lets anyone build apps in plain language, and Autopilot, a long-running personal agent, is rolling out in Frontier.

Why it matters for professional services: Home, Workspaces and Autopilot let teams draft, analyze and keep client projects moving in one shared space.
Source: Jared Spataro, Microsoft Copilot Blog · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 2

Dynamics 365 in Copilot

What's new: Dynamics 365 now brings 57+ CRM skills for sales and service, and 39+ ERP skills, into Copilot. The Sales and ERP apps plugins are generally available, bringing account planning, deal insights, purchase orders and finance tasks into Copilot and Excel. Dynamics 365 data now flows into Work IQ, and Activate speeds moves from Salesforce, with SAP ECC in private preview.

Why it matters for professional services: Project, finance and sales skills in Copilot help partners plan accounts and track work without switching apps.
Source: Jeff Teper, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Blog · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Agent 365: FinOps for AI

What's new: Cost Management shows and controls spend on usage-based AI services with policies, limits and alerts. Business Analytics measures the value AI delivers, and new governance tools find inactive or ownerless agents.

Why it matters for professional services: Firms can see AI cost by practice and measure the value it adds to billable work.
Source: Book of News · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 4

Copilotforce: Salesforce in Copilot, Teams and Fabric

What's new: Microsoft and Salesforce announced a partnership. Salesforce in Copilot is in preview with 38+ sales skills that blend CRM data with context from Teams, Outlook and documents. Sales Cloud in Teams is in pilot, and Zero Copy links Salesforce Data 360 with Microsoft Fabric.

Why it matters for professional services: Accenture is the first pilot customer of Copilotforce, bringing Salesforce into Copilot for its own sales team.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 17
Share: LinkedIn · Email
