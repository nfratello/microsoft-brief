How sources become a Brief

Each week, a small team of AI agents in Microsoft Copilot Cowork reads these sources, keeps what is new, tags each story by product and industry, and writes it once in plain English. Each edition then picks the stories that matter to its readers. A person reviews every issue before it is sent.
