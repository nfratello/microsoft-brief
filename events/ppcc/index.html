What we heard

Six takeaways

Start with one process

The message all week: pick one process that matters, improve it, then scale what works.

Copilot-first apps

Sessions covered Copilot-first app design, multi-agent systems and agent evaluation.

Governed by design

Agents, apps, workflows and data working together as governed, secure solutions.

Customer proof

The NFL’s Game Ops Dashboard, built with Copilot Studio and Power Platform, saves its team 8 to 12 hours a week. Asurion lifted touchless invoice processing from under 20% to over 70%.

More than two million makers are active every month, running more than four million apps.
