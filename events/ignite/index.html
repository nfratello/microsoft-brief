What was announced

Ignite in four themes

The headlines that matter most, grouped the way Microsoft told the story. Each one is public, with its release status.

Human ambition

Copilot is where work gets done

  • Home and the Office apps in CopilotGenerally available

    Home brings Chat and Cowork together, and people can create and edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files inside the Copilot app. Auto picks the right model and effort for each task.

  • Copilot CodeFrontier program

    Anyone can build dashboards, trackers and apps in plain language. Projects run in a secure sandbox inside your tenant, with IT in control.

  • Autopilot and WorkspacesRolling out in Frontier

    Autopilot is a long-running personal agent with its own identity that follows up and keeps projects moving. Workspaces gives a team one shared space with Copilot.

Why it matters: People spend less time switching apps and more time on the work only they can do.

Intelligence

Your business context, built in

  • Microsoft IQGenerally available

    One platform that connects agents to how your people work, your business data and your content, through Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ and Web IQ.

  • Dynamics 365 and Salesforce in CopilotGenerally available and preview

    Dynamics 365 plugins bring more than 100 prebuilt sales, service and finance skills into Copilot. Salesforce in Copilot is in preview with 38+ sales skills.

  • Agents that improve in productionGenerally available

    Microsoft Foundry adds multi-turn evaluation, production insights and an agent optimizer, so agents get measurably better over time.

Why it matters: AI is only as useful as what it knows about your business. This is how it learns it, with your permissions intact.

Trust

Govern AI and see what it delivers

  • Agent 365: FinOps for AIAvailable and in Frontier

    Cost Management shows and controls spend on usage-based AI services with policies, limits and alerts. Business Analytics measures the value AI delivers.

  • Governance across every agentPreview

    New Agent 365 tools surface inactive or ownerless agents, classify what agents do and govern the plugins and tools they use.

  • Microsoft PerceptionGenerally available

    Red, blue and green team security agents find weaknesses, triage alerts and investigate attacks. The Triage Agent covers about 80% of alerts in a typical Defender queue.

Why it matters: Leaders can scale AI with confidence when they can see what it costs, what it does and what it returns.

Cloud and data

The foundation under it all

  • Azure Cobalt 200Generally available

    Microsoft's own Arm-based virtual machines deliver up to 50% better CPU performance than Cobalt 100, built for the work around AI agents.

  • Azure HorizonDBGenerally available

    A fully managed, PostgreSQL-compatible database with built-in vector search and AI features for mission-critical apps.

  • Azure CatalystGenerally available

    One agentic modernization experience across Azure and GitHub, from discovery and planning through migration.

Why it matters: Faster, more efficient infrastructure lowers the cost of running AI at scale.

Spotlight · Business applications

Dynamics 365 in Copilot: your business apps come to where you work

In his Ignite post, Jeff Teper, EVP of Microsoft Apps and Agents, describes the shift: the work and its business context now come to people in Copilot, Teams and Excel, with Dynamics 365 as the system of record behind them. It rests on three things.

ExperienceCopilot is the front door to your business, and plugins teach it how you work.
ContextMicrosoft IQ gives employees and agents an understanding of your business.
GovernanceAgent 365 secures, manages and optimizes AI and what it costs.

Sales plugin in CopilotGenerally available

Sellers build account plans, prep for meetings, catch up on deals and prioritize pipeline in Copilot, and build PowerPoint decks from deal data. With the Customer Service plugin, there are now 57+ customer experience skills.

In Microsoft's own sales team: more than 25% less effort on account planning, meeting prep and proposals, and 9% faster deal closure (internal study).

ERP apps plugin in Copilot and ExcelGenerally available

39+ finance and operations skills bring ERP context and actions into Copilot and Excel, such as creating requests for quotes and managing purchase orders.

Dynamics 365 data in Work IQNew

Update customer records or prepare journal entries in plain language, with existing permissions and governance respected.

Copilot Studio: build whole processesGenerally available

The new Copilot harness makes agents more capable and cheaper to run. App creation is generally available on Copilot Managed Runtime, and Agent Accounts enter public preview in December.

In Microsoft testing, multitool performance rose from 87.0% to 98.9% and input tokens per task fell 45%.

Contact Center IntelligenceGenerally available

Adds AI agents for voice and digital channels on top of the contact center platform you already run, with no rip and replace.

Dynamics 365 ActivateGenerally available and private preview

Agent-assisted migration from Salesforce to Dynamics 365 is generally available. For ERP, SAP ECC discovery, requirements, configuration and data migration are in private preview.

A partner reports one enterprise moving 20,000 users off Salesforce in about five months, versus the typical 12 to 18.

See it live in the keynote, then go deeper in the sessions below. Jeff's post also has an audio version you can listen to.

Also announced · Partnership

Copilotforce: Salesforce comes into Copilot, Teams and Fabric

Microsoft and Salesforce announced a partnership that brings Salesforce customer context, processes and actions into Microsoft Copilot, with the security and permissions customers already rely on. Supported updates back to the CRM always need the user's confirmation.

Salesforce in CopilotPreview

38+ prebuilt sales skills, such as pipeline review, close planning, deal review, customer health and call follow-up, that combine Salesforce data with context from Teams meetings, Outlook and documents.

Sales Cloud in TeamsPilot

Pipeline and quota progress, deal notifications and briefing materials right inside Teams conversations. Open beta is planned for later this year.

Zero Copy with Microsoft FabricGenerally available and preview

Salesforce Data 360 can query Fabric OneLake data without moving it (generally available), and Data 360 customer data can be shared into OneLake (preview).

Accenture is the design and implementation partner, and its own global sales organization is the first pilot customer. For teams that run both Salesforce and Microsoft 365, sellers work from one place.
