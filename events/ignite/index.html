Know before you go

Six things worth knowing

💻

Can’t travel? Watch free

The online pass gives free access to keynotes and a wide range of sessions. No travel required.

🎓

Free certification exam

In-person attendees can take one Microsoft or GitHub certification exam free onsite. Reserve a slot.

🧪

Reserve pre-day early

Pre-day labs on Nov 16 are booked separately and some are already full.

🪪

Faster check-in

Digital Pass is an optional facial check-in that can make registration up to 3x faster.

🗂️

Topics to follow

Agentic business apps, agent platform, Copilot, Microsoft IQ, data, security, Windows and industry.

🧭

Plan your sessions

Use the session catalog and save favourites before you arrive. Times may change.
