Know before you go

Six things worth knowing

Register early

Passes start at $1,999 (+tax). The event sold out in 2025 and is already 80% full.

Where to stay

The Fontainebleau group block closed Nov 6. Hotel Continental and Radisson Resort Miami Beach also hold Convergence blocks.

Getting there

Fly into Miami International (MIA). A taxi or rideshare to the resort runs about $50. Valet is $65 a night.

Workshops need prep

Friday workshops need a prepared environment in your own tenant. Setup details come after you register.

Check-in times

Hotel check-in is 4:00 pm and check-out is 11:00 am. Luggage can be held free if you arrive early.

Beware housing scams

Book only through the official site. The organisers never use third-party housing companies.
