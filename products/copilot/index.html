A personal agent that keeps work moving

✨What's new

The October update brings Chat and Cowork together in one Copilot Home and introduces Autopilot, an always-on personal agent with its own account, mailbox and files, rolling out to Frontier customers in November.

💡Why it matters

Copilot moves from answering prompts to carrying ongoing work forward, while people keep control of the decisions that matter.
