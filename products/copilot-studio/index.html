What's new, and why it matters

Newest first. Each one links to the official announcement and the Brief edition it appeared in.

Agents Nov 9, 2026

Agents that finish long, complex tasks

What's new: A Copilot Studio deep dive reports agents on the Microsoft Copilot harness passed 78% to 89% of evaluation tasks, and kept working after a failed step in 84% to 91% of cases.

Why it matters: Long, multistep processes are more likely to finish without being handed back to a person.

Platform Nov 4, 2026

Build agents from the tools you already use

What's new: October's update adds a Copilot Studio MCP server for tools such as GitHub Copilot and makes Work IQ generally available in Copilot Studio. Cost controls for agents are in preview.

Why it matters: Makers can build where they already work, and admins get central control over agent spending.

Read the announcement → ✦ Ask Copilot what this means for us
Agents Oct 28, 2026

A stronger engine for your agents

What's new: The harness in Copilot Studio is now the Microsoft Copilot harness. Existing agents and billing don't change, and internal tests showed pass rates up 10.8 to 22.1 points over the standard engine.

Why it matters: Improvements to the engine Microsoft uses for its own Copilots now reach your agents too.

Read the announcement → ✦ Ask Copilot what this means for us
Customer service Oct 21, 2026

Voice and chat agents for your customers

What's new: A new customer conversation harness, in public preview, is built for customer-facing agents across voice and chat, with specialized sub-agents and handoff to people.

Why it matters: You can replace rigid phone menus with agents that keep context across channels.

Read the announcement → ✦ Ask Copilot what this means for us
Agents Oct 15, 2026

Agents get their own accounts and memory

What's new: Agents can now work in Microsoft 365 with their own accounts, be @mentioned in Teams and Outlook, remember business context and start work from events or schedules.

Why it matters: Recurring processes can be handed to agents, with a clear record of what each agent did.
