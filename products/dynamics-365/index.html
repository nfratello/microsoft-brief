ERP Nov 6, 2026

A second analyst nod for Dynamics 365 ERP

What's new: Gartner named Microsoft a Leader in its 2026 Magic Quadrant for Cloud ERP for Product-Centric Enterprises, highlighting the Procurement Agent and Copilot working on supply chain data.

Why it matters: Planners and buyers get agents that flag at-risk orders when suppliers slip dates, which helps with lead-time, tariff and labor pressure.

ERP Nov 3, 2026

Carbon data moves next to your finance data

What's new: Microsoft Sustainability Manager becomes Dynamics 365 Sustainability inside the ERP portfolio, tracking carbon, water and waste and calculating Scope 1–3 emissions with supplier data.

Why it matters: Emissions sit beside finance and sourcing decisions, which simplifies disclosure reporting and greener supplier choices.

ERP Nov 2, 2026

Rent, service and track equipment in one system

What's new: Rental Management is in public preview in Supply Chain Management, with linked rental features in Field Service and Commerce for one connected asset lifecycle.

Why it matters: Equipment makers and distributors can add rental and service revenue on the ERP they already run, tracking each unit's use, maintenance and profit without bolt-on systems.

CRM Oct 29, 2026

Your CRM, ready for any agent

What's new: The Dynamics 365 Sales MCP server is generally available, giving agents 59 governed tools for leads, opportunities, quotes and orders under your existing security roles.

Why it matters: Sellers can update CRM and turn quotes into orders from Copilot or their own agents, with no custom integration and no change to security.

CRM Oct 21, 2026

AI agents for the contact center you already run

What's new: Microsoft Contact Center Intelligence is in public preview. It adds AI self-service, quality-review agents and voice authentication on top of existing contact centers, including Five9, Cisco and Avaya.

Why it matters: Service leaders can cut wait times and review every call for quality without a platform migration.
