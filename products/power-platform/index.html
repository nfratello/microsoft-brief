9,000+ makers, and apps come to Copilot Studio

What's new: More than 9,000 people attended the Power Platform Community Conference, where apps in Copilot Studio were shown in preview. Power Apps daily users rose 20% over the past year.

Data Oct 26, 2026

Agents can run your business logic safely

What's new: Agents can now call Dataverse custom APIs through the Dataverse MCP Server, so multistep operations run as one transaction.

Why it matters: Agents use your existing business rules without leaving half-finished records when a step fails.

What's new: A Power Platform admin plugin for Copilot Cowork, in preview, answers plain-language questions about environments, usage, governance, capacity and licensing.

Data Oct 23, 2026

Copilot learns your business data

What's new: Business applications in Work IQ, in preview, give agents a shared understanding of your Dataverse data, with reusable business skills and natural-language questions.

Why it matters: Agents can reason over your records and rules instead of rebuilding that context every time.

Build apps with AI, manage them in Power Apps
