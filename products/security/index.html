What's new, and why it matters

Newest first. Each one links to the official announcement and the Brief edition it appeared in.

AI security Nov 5, 2026

Agents get only the access a task needs

What's new: New Dynamic Permissions give an agent authority scoped to its current task for only as long as needed, and Microsoft Execution Containers, a Windows containment layer for AI agents, is generally available.

Why it matters: You can limit what AI agents can do before one goes beyond its job.

Threat protection Oct 29, 2026

See every AI agent on your devices

What's new: Microsoft Defender can now discover local AI agents and their MCP servers on Windows and macOS, covering 50+ agents. Protection against prompt injection remains in preview.

Why it matters: Security teams can see which desktop agents are running and what they can reach.

Read the announcement → ✦ Ask Copilot what this means for us
Security operations Oct 20, 2026

Run security across many tenants

What's new: Cross-tenant delegated administration in Microsoft Defender is generally available, extending to Sentinel with granular permissions.

Why it matters: Central security teams and managed providers can work across tenants with least privilege and no extra guest accounts.

Read the announcement → ✦ Ask Copilot what this means for us
Security operations Oct 20, 2026

Sentinel data, health and cost in one view

What's new: Unified Data Management for Microsoft Sentinel, in preview, brings connectors, tables, ingestion health and estimated costs into one view in the Defender portal.

Why it matters: Security teams can spot data gaps and cost spikes before they affect detection.

Identity Oct 12, 2026

Access adjusts the moment a device is lost

What's new: Entra's continuous access checks now use user-risk and device signals, in preview, and a guided migration to Entra Cloud Sync is generally available.

Why it matters: Access can change quickly when a device is lost or out of compliance, and hybrid identity gets simpler.
