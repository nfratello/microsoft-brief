This week's stories

 

Media's real problem is scattered data, not too little

What's new: Microsoft's US telco, media and gaming lead argues media companies should connect content, ad, commerce and rights data under governance they trust, citing CAA, Kantar and the NBA on Fabric and Azure.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Broadcasters and streamers keep audience signals in separate systems. Connecting them is the quickest way to turn an AI pilot into revenue.
Source: The Microsoft Cloud Blog · Oct 15
 

Lumen and Microsoft team up on AI-ready networks

What's new: Lumen and Microsoft will plan enterprise accounts together where network upgrades, Azure migration and AI meet. Lumen intends to bring its cloud-networking suite to Azure, and Alkira is already on Microsoft Marketplace.

Why it matters for telecom and media: Operators are becoming the layer that connects data spread across clouds and sites for AI. Joint go-to-market with Azure gives them a direct route into enterprise AI budgets.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 2
