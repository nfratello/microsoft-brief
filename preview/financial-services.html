This week's stories

 

Four places AI agents can change insurance

What's new: After ITC Vegas, Microsoft named four priorities for insurers: underwriting, real-time actuarial risk, claims and relationship managers, citing Shift Technology, Gallagher and Generali France.

Why it matters for financial services: Insurers get a practical map for scaling agents with clear authority limits and escalation paths. Governance is what lets them move faster.
Source: The Microsoft Cloud Blog · Oct 13
 

Northern Trust cuts onboarding data prep from weeks to days

What's new: An agent built on Azure and Microsoft Foundry converts new clients' transaction history into Northern Trust's format. Prep time fell 83%, from about 2.5 weeks to three days, with analysts reviewing in Excel and SharePoint.

Why it matters for financial services: A workable pattern for regulated firms: the agent was tuned only on anonymised data, client data stayed under the bank's control, and humans approve every output.
Source: Microsoft Source · Nov 9
