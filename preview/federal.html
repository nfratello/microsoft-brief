1 big thing

Government clouds get commercial-grade Copilot models

What's new: GPT-5.6 is now in GCC, GCC High and DoD. Edit with Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint and Copilot in Forms reach all three clouds; scheduling and a simpler app design are rolling out.

Why it matters for federal: Federal employees have been working with older AI models than commercial users. This narrows the gap inside the Office apps agencies rely on every day.
Source: Microsoft Public Sector Blog · Nov 3

Story 2

Azure Enclave launches for sensitive government work

What's new: A managed service in Azure and Azure for US Government that sets up isolated, policy-governed environments where agencies can work with labs, contractors and academic partners.

Why it matters for federal: Agencies start from a ready-made isolation design instead of custom builds. FedRAMP, IL4–6 and ICD 503 accreditation is still in progress.
Source: The Microsoft Cloud Blog · Oct 13

One move for Program management · Federal Government

Boil a long report down to a one-page brief

Leaders need the findings and the decisions, not 40 pages. Use only your agency's approved Copilot environment.

Copy this prompt
I've attached a 40-page program report. Write a one-page brief for leadership: 3 key findings, 3 risks, and 3 decisions we need, each with the page number it came from.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
