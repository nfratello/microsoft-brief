1 big thing

Inside Unilever's AI factory work

What's new: Microsoft Frontier Company has engaged on nearly 500 projects with over 200 customers in 28 countries. Examples include a factory digital twin at Unilever, an order-management agent at 3M, and AIO-Net at KT.

Why it matters for retail: Unilever, P&G and Land O'Lakes are among the customers named. Consumer goods makers can use digital twins to catch issues before they hit output and shelves.

Story 2

Same AI work, very different monthly bills

What's new: Microsoft-funded research with GigaOm compared the total cost of Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise and Claude Enterprise on the same tasks. On the modeled workloads, Copilot cost $30 a month for an everyday AI user, with broadly similar quality.

Why it matters for retail: Retailers with large store and support teams could give more people access to AI if everyday use stays inside a fixed subscription.
Source: Microsoft Copilot Blog · Nov 16
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 
Story 3

Frontier Firms see 4x returns on AI

What's new: New IDC research, commissioned by Microsoft, surveyed more than 5,200 leaders across 28 industries. Frontier Firms report an average 4x return on AI, versus 2.7x for slow adopters.

Why it matters for retail: Retail and consumer goods leaders can compare their spending, governance and skilling with the firms seeing the biggest returns.
Source: The Official Microsoft Blog · Nov 12
