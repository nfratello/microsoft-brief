Microsoft 365 Copilot

Copilot for legal is now generally available

What's new: Microsoft released 20+ legal skills for Copilot: drafting, playbook review, tracked redlines, obligation tracking and case chronologies, plus plugins for iManage, NetDocuments, Harvey and Relativity.

Why it matters for professional services: Law firms already work in Word. Legal-grade AI that edits as native tracked changes and connects to document systems means less switching and faster first drafts.

Microsoft 365 Copilot

New Copilot skills for accountants and deal teams

What's new: Microsoft added 25+ finance skills and plugins for reconciliations, journal entries, audit, tax, valuation models and investment memos. In Excel, users can turn on an audit trail.
