This week's stories

 

Agents that remember and start work on their own

What's new: Copilot Studio added agent accounts you can @-mention in Teams and Outlook (private preview), business process memory and triggers, plus Copilot Studio skills in Cowork.

Why it matters: Agents can own an ongoing process instead of waiting for a prompt, and business staff can build them in plain language.
Source: Microsoft Copilot Studio Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Carbon data moves next to your finance data

What's new: Microsoft Sustainability Manager becomes Dynamics 365 Sustainability inside the ERP portfolio, tracking carbon, water and waste and calculating Scope 1–3 emissions with supplier data.

Why it matters: Emissions sit beside finance and sourcing decisions, which simplifies disclosure reporting and greener supplier choices.
Source: Dynamics 365 Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Production-ready building blocks for reliable agents

What's new: Foundry's October roundup confirms GPT-6.1 Terra, the Decisions API for structured yes/no, choice and score answers, and long-running hosted-agent resilience are generally available.

Why it matters: Cheaper, structured decision steps make agents more predictable in production.
Source: Microsoft Foundry Blog
Share: LinkedIn · Email
 

Give every agent only the access its task needs

What's new: Dynamic Permissions scope an agent's authority to its current task, and Microsoft Execution Containers for OS-level agent containment are generally available.

Why it matters: Practical controls to limit what AI agents can do, reducing exposure without slowing people down.
Source: Microsoft Security Blog
