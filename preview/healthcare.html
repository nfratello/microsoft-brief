1 big thing
SickKidsCustomer

Top children's hospital makes Azure its primary cloud

What's new: Under a five-year deal, SickKids is making Azure its main cloud for clinical systems, including the EHR. Microsoft adds Azure credits, partner support and AI skills training.

Why it matters for healthcare: It is a model for providers: get the data foundation right first, then use AI for faster diagnosis and more personalised care.

Story 2
Boehringer IngelheimCustomer

Digital patient twins could make drug trials faster

What's new: Boehringer Ingelheim and Microsoft will research Digital Patient Twins, starting with lung health: disease-progression modelling, trial simulation, and synthetic or hybrid control arms.

Why it matters for healthcare: If it succeeds, fewer patients would be needed in control groups and trial evidence would come together faster. That could bring therapies to market sooner.

Turn a 20-page policy into a one-page checklist

Long policies rarely get read at the start of a shift. A checklist does. Always have a clinical lead review the result.
