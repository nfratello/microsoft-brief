This week's stories

 
Azure

Stop methane leaks before they start

What's new: Microsoft's energy lead describes how connected data and predictive models turn methane alerts into prioritized work orders, verified repairs and audit-ready records, with people keeping key decisions.

Why it matters for energy: Leaks waste product and create regulatory risk. Moving from finding leaks to preventing them protects margins, safety and reporting credibility.

Azure

Use governed energy data in your analytics tools

What's new: Native analytics in Azure Data Manager for Energy is generally available. Selected data is shared in an open format that works with Microsoft Fabric, Azure Databricks, Snowflake and similar platforms.

Why it matters for energy: Energy companies no longer rebuild subsurface data for every AI project. Analysts and engineers use trusted data in the tools they already prefer.
