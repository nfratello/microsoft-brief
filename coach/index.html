The prompt formula

Microsoft's own guidance: a good prompt has four parts.

Goal

What you want. "Summarize the variances…"

Context

Why, and for whom. "…for my CFO update…"

Details

Format and limits. "…as a table, top 5…"

Data

What to use. "…from the attached spend file."
