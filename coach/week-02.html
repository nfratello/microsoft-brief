Better prompts, better answers

 
    

Smart Brevity count: 657 words · 3 min

Morning. Last week was one habit: brief Copilot like a sharp colleague. This week we make it concrete. Four small moves turn a vague answer into one you can use.

Everything here works in Copilot Chat. Where Microsoft 365 Copilot adds more, I say so.

 
 

Here is a prompt most of us have typed, and the same prompt after four small moves.

Before: a vague answer
Summarize the budget.
After: a useful answer
I'm presenting to our leadership team on Thursday. Using the attached Q3 budget file, summarize spend against plan for each department, flag the 3 biggest overruns with a likely reason, and finish with 2 questions leadership will probably ask. Use a table and keep it under 200 words.

Works in Copilot Chat when you attach the file.

 
Goal. Say what you want, in one sentence. "Summarize spend against plan…"
Context. Say who it is for and why. "…I'm presenting to leadership on Thursday."
Details. Set the format, length and tone. "…a table, under 200 words."
Data. Point to the source. Attach it, or type / and the name of a file, person or meeting. "…using the attached Q3 budget file."

You don't need all four every time. If an answer misses, ask which part was missing and add it.

 

Microsoft's own advice: don't settle for the first answer. Copilot remembers the conversation, so a short follow-up is often enough:

Make it shorter: "Cut this to 5 bullets for a busy executive."
Change the shape: "Turn this into a table with owner and due date."
Check the gaps: "What's missing, or what would a skeptic ask?"
Let Copilot ask: "Ask me 3 questions one at a time, then rewrite it."
 
One move for Risk & compliance · Financial Services

Get a plain-English brief on new guidance

Regulatory updates are long and dense. Start with a short brief and a list of questions, then go to the source.

Copy this prompt
Using the web, summarize the latest public guidance on AI model risk management for banks in 5 bullets. List what it could change for a mid-size bank, and draft 3 questions for our compliance team. Link every source.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat with web answers. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
