Your first five minutes with Copilot Chat

Morning. This week is about one habit: asking Copilot the way you'd brief a sharp colleague. Five minutes, three prompts, one thing to try today.

Everyone with a work account can start with Copilot Chat. If your company has Microsoft 365 Copilot, it can also use your email, meetings and files. I've marked which is which.

 
 

Open Copilot and you'll see three tabs and a switch:

Quick questions, summaries and drafts. Start here.
Longer, multi-step work you hand off and get back finished.
Describe a small tool or automation and Copilot builds it.
On: answers can use your work files, email and meetings. Off: only the web.

Your screen may differ: Microsoft is still rolling out the new Home view, and features depend on your license and company settings.

 

Brief Copilot like a sharp new colleague

Short prompts get generic answers. Microsoft's guidance is to give four things:

GoalWhat you want. "Compare actual spend to plan…"
ContextWhy, and for whom. "…for my CFO update…"
DetailsFormat and limits. "…top 5 variances, as a table…"
DataWhat to use. "…from the attached spend file."

Two pro moves: type / and a name to point Copilot at a specific file, person or meeting. And don't settle for the first answer: ask it to shorten, change the format or go deeper.

 
1. Catch up on a long document
Summarize this document in 5 bullets, then list any decisions or deadlines it mentions.
Where it works: Copilot Chat: attach the file
2. Draft the email you've been putting off
Help me draft an email to [person] about [topic]. Ask me 3 questions one at a time first, then write it in under 150 words.
Where it works: Copilot Chat
3. See what needs you this week
What should be on my radar from last week? List the 5 things that need me, who is waiting, and by when.
Where it works: Microsoft 365 Copilot: reads your email and meetings
 
One move for Finance · Energy & Utilities

Find the outliers in maintenance spend

Variance reviews eat days. Let Copilot find the big gaps first so you spend your time on the why.

Copy this prompt
I've attached this quarter's maintenance spend by site and our plan. Compare actual to plan, list the 5 biggest variances with likely reasons, and format it as a table I can paste into my CFO update.

Where it works: Works in Copilot Chat: paste or attach your file. Why it works: it gives Copilot a goal, context, details and the data to use.

New in Copilot: Look for three tabs in the Copilot app: Chat for quick questions and drafts, Cowork for longer work you hand off, and Code for building small tools. A Work IQ switch decides whether answers use your work data or only the web.
